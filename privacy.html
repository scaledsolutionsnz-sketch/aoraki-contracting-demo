Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how Aoraki Contracting of Twizel, New Zealand handles personal information. This page contains placeholder content for the purposes of this demonstration website and should be replaced with the business's own policy before going live.

Information we collect

When you contact us by phone or email we may collect your name, contact details, property address and details about the work you are enquiring about. We only collect information that is needed to respond to you and to carry out the job.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To plan, schedule and complete contracted work
  • To invoice for completed work and keep business records

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is only shared where necessary to complete a job, for example with a subcontractor or supplier, or where required by law.

Storage and security

Personal information is stored securely and kept only as long as needed for business and legal purposes.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted.

Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to Aoraki Contracting on 027 435 1588.
