About us

A local crew that knows the Mackenzie.

Aoraki Contracting is a Twizel based earthmoving and electrical contractor working across the Mackenzie Basin, from Tekapo to Omarama. Out here the ground is stony, the winters bite and tradespeople are a long drive away, so getting the job done properly on the first visit matters.

Because we handle both the digging and the wiring, jobs like shed power, water connections and new sections get done as one piece of work instead of a juggling act between contractors.
